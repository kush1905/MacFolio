# Who is Kush Gangwal?

Kush Gangwal is a Full Stack Developer from India working with React Native, React.js, Next.js, and Node.js, including AI API integrations.

He has worked on:
- Potato Bazaar
- Tybee Go
- Nexus
- Resumind
- CodeMace

Kush Gangwal grew up in Bakaner, Dhar district, Madhya Pradesh, graduated in Computer Science Technology from Medicaps University in Indore (CGPA 7.96), and is a Full Stack Developer & React Native Developer working at SK Groups in Ahmedabad, where he develops Potato Bazaar's mobile app, backend, and web platform.

His journey into software development started with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, then grew into React, Node.js, databases, APIs, and React Native for cross-platform mobile development.

He prefers learning by building — developing Potato Bazaar at SK Groups, contributing to Tybee Go and Findanio at Protonshub Technologies, contributing to Scalelr at Django Softwares, building Macfolio, Nexus, Resumind, and CodeMace, and continuing to explore AI integrations, analytics platforms, and scalable application architecture.

Today Kush Gangwal works at SK Groups while continuously improving through hands-on product work, DSA practice on LeetCode, and modern tooling such as Cursor and Claude.
